Problem

Churn predictions are only useful when teams can act on them.

Instead of stopping at a notebook metric, this project turns model output into a customer-success queue: who is at risk, why they are at risk, how much revenue is exposed, and which retention action should happen next.

What I built

ML pipeline

Data generation, preprocessing, training, model comparison, threshold tuning, drift scenario, and deployable JSON model bundle.

Product app

CSV upload, dynamic column mapping, live charts, scoring table, top risk drivers, action recommendations, and CRM/task export.

MLOps foundation

CI, Vercel deployment, serverless scoring APIs, Postgres-ready workflow schema, audit logs, model version tables, and optional API-token protection.

Architecture

1. TrainGenerate or load customer records, preprocess fields, train and compare models.
2. ExportSave a lightweight scoring bundle for Vercel serverless usage.
3. ScoreScore individual accounts or uploaded CSV batches with flexible column mapping.
4. ActRank customers by revenue at risk and generate recommended retention actions.
5. GovernTrack model metrics, drift signals, batches, outcomes, and audit-ready workflow state.

Practicality

Portfolio-ready now, production-ready foundation next.

The public demo runs safely in browser-first mode. For production, the repository already includes Postgres persistence APIs and schema; connecting a managed database through DATABASE_URL activates server-side saved batches and audit logs.
